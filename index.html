📘 Pearson Edexcel GCSE Spanish (1SP1) · 2024 specification

The six official thematic contexts are My personal world, Lifestyle and wellbeing, My neighbourhood, Media and technology, Studying and my future, and Travel and tourism. Grammar is taught across contexts. The original seven adventure topic cards below are legacy practice and have not been fully checked against the prescribed vocabulary list.

1SP1 • Official thematic context 1

👋 My personal world

Original GCSE-style practice focused on family, friends, relationships and opinions. Version 6 expands writing formats. This is not an official Pearson assessment or a complete prescribed-vocabulary audit.

Learning objectives

Understand family and relationship descriptions, recognise opinions and time references, write connected Spanish and practise spoken responses. Choose Foundation or Higher at the top of the page.
